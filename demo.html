Things to try

  1. On the dashboard below, find Night 20–08 and press Potential job, then Send potential job. Both phones get a normal notification and an amber banner. Tap SEEN on one.
  2. Press Confirm job · sound alarm. Each phone turns into a flashing red NEW JOB screen that sounds until ACKNOWLEDGE is tapped, and Pushsafer repeats the Critical Alert every 30 seconds. 2 of the 3 night crew must acknowledge: tap it on Sam and on Jo.
  3. Alert sent and Alert acknowledged fill in by themselves. Then tap the stages as the job goes on.
  4. Turn a phone's signal off and tap a stage. The phone keeps it and sends it when the signal comes back.
  5. Open All teams on a phone. Colours show each team's state: green available, blue on a job, amber no update for over an hour (Day 10–22 C), red alarm not answered.
  6. Finish the job, or tap End my shift to see the sign-on screen.

Click the dashboard once to allow its chime. Turn your sound on to hear the phone alarm.
